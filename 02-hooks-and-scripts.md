# 02 · Hooks & Reels scripts

> Short-form is won or lost in the first 1.5 seconds. These prompts force the AI to think in retention.

---

## 2.1 · Hook generator (10 angles)

```text
ROLE: Short-form video copywriter who has written scripts with 10MM+ views.
TOPIC: {{topic}} · FORMAT: {{Reel / TikTok, length}} · GOAL: {{saves / shares / comments / follows}}
TASK: Write 10 hooks, each using a different angle:
contrarian · mistake · curiosity gap · number · story start · "POV" · myth vs fact · before/after · direct callout of the viewer · question.
RULES: Max 12 words. Spoken language, not written. No clickbait the video can't deliver.
OUTPUT: Table → hook | angle | on-screen text (max 6 words) | why it stops the scroll. Then mark your top 3.
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 2.2 · 30-second Reel script with timestamps

```text
Write a 30s Reel script about {{topic}} for {{brand}}.
Structure: 0-2s hook · 2-8s tension/problem · 8-22s value (max 3 points) · 22-27s payoff · 27-30s CTA ({{CTA}}).
For each block give: voiceover, on-screen text, b-roll/shot suggestion.
Rules: one idea per Reel, sentences under 10 words, include one pattern interrupt around second 10.
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 2.3 · Storytelling Reel (personal brand)

```text
Turn this real experience into a 45s storytelling Reel: {{story in raw words}}
Use the structure: situation → conflict → turning point → lesson → bridge to my offer (soft).
Keep my voice from the Brand Voice Card. Give 2 versions: emotional and humorous.
Add 3 caption options (short, medium, long) and a pinned comment.
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 2.4 · Long video → 8 clips

```text
Here is the transcript of a {{YouTube video / podcast}}: {{transcript}}
Find the 8 best moments for short clips. For each: start/end quote, why it works standalone, a new hook to put before it, on-screen title, and the platform it fits best (IG / TikTok / Shorts / LinkedIn).
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 2.5 · Trend adapter

```text
Trend: {{describe audio/format}}. Brand: see voice card.
Give 3 ways to adapt it that a) make sense for our audience, b) carry a real message, c) don't look forced.
Score each from 1-5 on brand fit and production effort. Tell me honestly if we should skip this trend.
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 2.6 · Retention doctor

```text
This Reel had {{X}}% average watch time and drop-off at second {{Y}}. Script: {{script}}
Diagnose why people left, then rewrite the first 10 seconds in 3 different ways to fix it. Explain the change in one line each.
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 2.7 · Series concept (recurring format)

```text
Create 3 recurring video series concepts for {{brand}} that we can produce weekly in under {{time}}.
For each: series name, format rules, episode structure, 6 episode ideas, why it builds habit, and the KPI to judge it after 4 episodes.
Respond in {{language}}.
```
