# 05 · Paid social (Meta & TikTok Ads)

> Built from managing 25K €/year in Meta & TikTok Ads at a 0.25 € CPA. Creative is the new targeting.

---

## 5.1 · Ad angle matrix

```text
ROLE: Performance creative strategist.
Product: {{product + price}} · Audience: {{audience}} · Objective: {{conversions / leads / traffic}}
TASK: Build an angle matrix: 6 angles (pain, desire, objection-killer, social proof, authority, comparison).
For each: hook (spoken), primary text (≤125 chars), headline (≤40 chars), visual concept, best placement (Reels / Feed / TikTok).
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 5.2 · UGC brief for creators

```text
Write a UGC brief for {{product}} to send to {{N}} creators.
Include: goal, key message (1 sentence), 3 mandatory points, 3 hook options, do's & don'ts, technical specs (9:16, ≤30s, subtitles), and 2 example scripts.
Keep it to one page.
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 5.3 · Testing plan

```text
Budget: {{€/day}} for {{days}}. Current CPA: {{€}}. Target CPA: {{€}}.
Design a creative testing plan: what to test first (hook vs format vs offer), how many variants, budget per ad set, when to kill/scale (clear rules with numbers), and a naming convention.

```

## 5.4 · Organic post → ad

```text
This organic post performed above average: {{post + metrics}}.
Explain why it worked, then adapt it into 3 ad variants (keep what worked, add a clear offer and CTA). Note which ad policy risks to avoid ({{niche}}).
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 5.5 · Ad performance diagnosis

```text
Campaign data: {{paste: spend, impressions, CPM, CTR, CPC, conversions, CPA, frequency per ad}}
Diagnose each ad: is the problem the hook (low thumbstop/CTR), the offer (good CTR, bad CVR), the audience (high CPM) or fatigue (frequency)?
Output: table with diagnosis + specific next action per ad. End with the 1 decision I should take today.
Respond in {{language}}.
```
