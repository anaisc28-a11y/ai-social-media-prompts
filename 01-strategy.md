# 01 · Strategy & positioning

> Paste your [Brand Voice Card](../brand-voice-card.md) before any of these prompts.

---

## 1.1 · Profile audit in 10 minutes

```text
ROLE: You are a senior social media strategist who audits personal brands and small businesses.
CONTEXT: Below is my Instagram bio, my last 12 posts (format + caption + reach/saves/comments) and my main offer.
{{paste data}}
TASK: Audit the profile like a potential client would see it in the first 5 seconds, then like an algorithm would.
RULES: Be direct. No generic advice. Every finding must point to a specific post or line.
OUTPUT:
1. First impression score (0-10) + why
2. Table: what works / what doesn't / evidence
3. Bio rewrite: 3 options (max 150 characters each)
4. Top 3 changes ranked by impact vs effort
CHECK: Before answering, remove any recommendation that could apply to any account.
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 1.2 · Content pillars from real audience pain

```text
ROLE: Brand strategist specialised in {{niche}}.
CONTEXT: These are real comments, DMs and questions from my audience: {{paste 20-50}}
TASK: Cluster them into pains, desires and objections, then design 3-4 content pillars.
OUTPUT: For each pillar → name, the pain it solves, % of the content mix, 5 post ideas, the KPI it should move (reach / saves / DMs / sales).
RULES: Use the audience's own words in the post ideas. At least one pillar must build trust (story / proof).
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 1.3 · Audience persona that is actually useful

```text
Create 2 personas for {{brand}} based ONLY on the data below (no invented demographics).
Data: {{insights from Instagram, survey answers, customer reviews}}
For each persona: a day in their life, what they search at 23:00, the sentence they would never say out loud, what makes them trust a brand, what makes them unfollow, and 3 hooks that would stop their scroll.
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 1.4 · Competitor content teardown

```text
ROLE: Social media analyst.
CONTEXT: Top 10 posts (by engagement) of 3 competitors: {{paste caption + format + metrics}}
TASK: Find the patterns behind their best content.
OUTPUT:
- Table: competitor | winning format | hook pattern | emotional trigger | CTA
- 3 gaps nobody is covering
- 3 ideas to do it better (not copy) for {{my brand}}
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 1.5 · 90-day social strategy (one page)

```text
Act as Head of Social. Build a one-page 90-day plan for {{brand}}.
Business goal: {{e.g. 40 sales of the 15 € audit}} · Current state: {{followers, avg reach, conversion}}
Include: north-star KPI, monthly milestones, content mix by pillar and format, weekly cadence the team can sustain with {{hours/week}}, paid budget split if any ({{€}}), and the 3 experiments to run each month.
Format: a table per month + a short "what we stop doing" list.
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 1.6 · Positioning statement for a personal brand

```text
Interview me with 8 questions (one at a time) about my expertise, my clients and my story.
When finished, write:
1. Positioning statement: "I help {{who}} achieve {{what}} through {{how}}, without {{pain}}."
2. 3 versions of my bio
3. My "origin story" in 120 words for a pinned Reel
4. 5 topics only I can talk about credibly
Respond in {{language}}.
```
