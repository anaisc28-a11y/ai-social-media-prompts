# 04 · Community & DMs

> AI can draft replies. A human should always send them.

---

## 4.1 · Reply drafts for a batch of comments

```text
Here are {{N}} comments from our last post: {{paste}}
Draft replies in our brand voice. Rules: personal (use details from each comment), max 2 sentences, ask a follow-up question when it can open a conversation, never copy-paste the same reply.
Flag with ⚠️ any comment that needs a human decision (complaint, medical question, legal, sensitive).
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 4.2 · DM keyword flow

```text
People comment "{{keyword}}" to receive {{lead magnet / info}}.
Write the DM flow: message 1 (deliver + 1 question), message 2 depending on answer A/B/C, message 3 soft offer of {{offer}}, and a follow-up after 48h if no reply.
Make it feel like a conversation, not a funnel.
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 4.3 · Negative comment / mini-crisis

```text
Situation: {{describe the comment/situation, how many people are reacting}}
Give me: severity level (1-5), whether to reply publicly, privately or both, 3 reply options (empathetic, factual, firm), what NOT to say, and an internal checklist for the next 24h.
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 4.4 · FAQ mining

```text
From these DMs and comments from the last month: {{paste}}
Extract the 10 most frequent questions, the exact words people use, and the objection behind each.
For each, suggest: a saved reply, a Story highlight topic and a piece of content that answers it before people ask.
Respond in {{language}}.
```

## 4.5 · Community ritual

```text
Propose 4 weekly community rituals for {{brand}} (e.g. Monday Q&A box, Friday wins) that increase replies and DMs.
For each: format, sticker, how long it takes us, how to reuse the answers as content.
Respond in {{language}}.
```
