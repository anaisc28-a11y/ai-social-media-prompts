# 🎙️ Brand Voice Card

> Fill this in once per brand. Paste it at the top of every AI chat (or save it as project instructions).
> *Rellénala una vez por marca y pégala al inicio de cada chat.*

```text
=== BRAND VOICE CARD ===

BRAND:            {{name + one-line description}}
WHO WE HELP:      {{specific audience — age, situation, main pain}}
PROMISE:          {{the transformation we deliver}}
LANGUAGE:         {{e.g. español (España), informal "tú"}}

PERSONALITY (3 words):   {{e.g. direct, warm, science-based}}
WE SOUND LIKE:           {{a friend who is a nutritionist, not a textbook}}
WE NEVER SOUND LIKE:     {{guru, salesy, preachy}}

WORDS WE USE:      {{list}}
WORDS WE AVOID:    {{list — e.g. "hack", "secret", "miracle"}}
EMOJIS:            {{none / max 2 per post / brand set: 🌿💪}}

CONTENT PILLARS:
  1. {{pillar}} — {{% of content}}
  2. {{pillar}} — {{%}}
  3. {{pillar}} — {{%}}

PROOF WE CAN USE:  {{results, numbers, testimonials, credentials}}
OFFERS:            {{products/services + price + link keyword}}
MAIN CTA:          {{e.g. comment "PLAN" / link in bio / DM}}

3 POSTS THAT WORKED (paste caption + metric):
  - {{...}}
  - {{...}}
  - {{...}}
=== END ===
```

## ✅ Filled example (wellness professional)

```text
BRAND:            Laura Fit — online coach for women 30–45 who want to get strong without diets
WHO WE HELP:      Busy mums who have tried 10 diets and feel guilty about food
PROMISE:          Strength + food peace in 12 weeks, 3 workouts a week
LANGUAGE:         español (España), tú, cercano
PERSONALITY:      honest, warm, evidence-based
WE SOUND LIKE:    the friend who studied sports science and tells you the truth
WE NEVER SOUND LIKE: before/after body shaming, "summer body" pressure
WORDS WE AVOID:   "quemar", "detox", "culpa", "pecar"
EMOJIS:           max 2, from 💪🌿✨
CONTENT PILLARS:  1. Strength training made simple (40%) 2. Food without guilt (30%) 3. My story & clients (30%)
PROOF:            +300 clients, certified ISSA trainer, 4.9★ reviews
OFFERS:           12-week program (149 €) — keyword "FUERTE"
MAIN CTA:         comment "FUERTE" to receive the program info by DM
```
